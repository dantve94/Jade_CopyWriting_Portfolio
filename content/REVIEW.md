# Content review checklist

The copy in `samples/` was extracted from the live pages linked on Clippings.me (November 2026). I tidied the **formatting only**: consistent front matter, plus long paragraphs that had been tagged as headings turned into normal text. **No wording has been changed.** Everything below is for Jade to decide.

## 1. Fill in for every sample

The `role` and `result` fields are blank on all 9 samples. Hiring managers look for these first (see `docs/design-research.md`, section 1).

- **role**: what Jade wrote, e.g. "Full homepage + service pages", "Blog post, concept to final".
- **result**: any metric, client feedback, or outcome. Leave blank if unknown; don't invent one.
- **published** (date, `YYYY-MM-DD`): missing on 02, 03, 07, 08, 09.

## 2. Confirm the live copy is still Jade's

Client websites get edited after handover. Check each website sample against Jade's original files and remove any sections she didn't write.

- **02 Parenting Collective:** this page mentions "90+ podcast episodes" and has a long first-person "Learning Hub" section in Donna's voice, so it may have been updated or partly written by someone else. Mark which sections are Jade's.
- **01 SuperSleeper:** the opening line "To where it all began – with innovative design…" reads like it may have been edited. Compare it with the original.
- **03 D'or Hairstylists:** the services list and product blurbs may be template or shop copy.

## 3. Proofreading flags

These errors are in the live copy. A reviewer will judge a copywriter on them, so either fix them in the portfolio version (noting it's the "as written" version if the live site differs) or choose a different excerpt.

| Sample | Current text | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|
| 02 | "over 21 years experience" | "over 21 years' experience" |
| 02 | "so is their sleep requirements" | "so are their sleep requirements" |
| 02 | "life time access" / "Life Time access" | "lifetime access" |

## 4. Curation suggestions

- **Featured** (`featured: true`): 02 Parenting Collective, 03 D'or Hairstylists, 04 The Blame Game. These are the strongest client pieces with a clear voice. Change this to suit.
- **Short stories** are marked `type: "personal"` so the site can label them "Personal work" and show them in their own section, after the client work. They show range and voice.
- **09 Her Whisper** contains sensual and violent content. It's fine for a creative-writing section, but consider leaving it off the version sent to corporate or B2B employers.
- **06 5 Reasons…** (2022) is the weakest sample. Consider dropping it or swapping in something newer.
- **06** ends with a named DC Lab staff member's email and phone number. Consider cutting that sentence from the portfolio version.
- **Missing formats:** no email, social, or ad samples yet. If Jade has any, they'd round out the portfolio.

## 5. Bio

`bio.md` is the Clippings.me bio. For the new site, a sharper one-line positioning statement would help, e.g.:

> Senior copywriter with 10+ years writing websites, blogs and brand stories that sound like your brand and bring the right people closer.
