### Her Whisper

He wanted her. Of course he couldn't say that to the crew. He was there to make sure she didn't escape. So that it wasn't like the last time. He didn't like it, guarding her that is. He was afraid, he was confused, he was also not exactly afraid. They were drinking again, the crew. He heard "Captain! Captain! Catch this!" and the occasional laugh. Whatever they were doing it wasn't fair. He'd already been through so much the least they could do was allow him to let loose a little. He deserved some escapism.

He could leave the cage alone just for a little bit, right? She wasn't going anywhere, right? What would he even do if she decided to attack again? Would it even be so bad? the thought arose. He didn't know where it came from but he tried not to listen. "Pass the mead!" he heard, muffled, from above. That's it! He was drinking too!

He snuck onto the deck making his way to the bow of the boat. That's where the keg was. The crew were so distracted in their debauchery they hardly noticed him. He grabbed the biggest vessel he could and filled it to the brim. Slamming back into the cage to slide to the ground, he sipped and sipped before he realised it had all fallen into complete silence.

"Hello, sailor..." she whispered behind his ear from inside the cage. His nerves met his skin, his hair standing to attention. Ready to salute, to worship. He dare not turn around. She knew the ways of delusion, she knew weakness and she knew seduction.

The storm had cleared. That's why they were messing around above deck. "You could show them..." she said. A sound that you imagine might catch the morning light. This was her trick he told himself but still, as well as he knew that, he looked at the lock of her cage. It glistened more than he had remembered, like he was looking at it for the first time. How strong was it? Did he even have the key or did he leave it by the keg?

He slipped his hand into his pocket and there it was. The key. Surely she should have gotten it already? Maybe she didn't want to escape. Was it the right key? Would it fit? He asked all these questions as he leaned toward the lock. He wanted to touch it just to feel its wetness on his skin. He knew what she was! A creature of the sea, a seducer of sailors, an omen, a beast, a breath-taking vision that he could simply not look away from. The crew must never know. She was also leaning against the cage like he was. She wasn't looking at him, or rather she was pretending not to look at him. Suddenly he felt it, her cold fingertips against the back of his neck. How could he possibly have thought that she would never touch him?

He couldn't help but look at her. She wasn't the half woman, half sea creature that she was when she was captured. She was another woman he had never laid his eyes on. So soft in the dim light coming through the wooden planks above her head. The merry sailors stamping around causing dust to fall onto her skin. Catching the little light there was, she looked like a vision.

Her legs where there were scales, looked soft to the touch. Her hips catching the shadows just enough to subtly say they were there. Her shining body reaching two breasts. He couldn't avert his eyes. He knew it was her illusion but did it even matter anymore? The curves of her breasts, her lips, the small of her neck. This was unlike anything he had ever seen.

He slipped his hand into his pocket again. "Just for a little" he said as he slowly brought the key towards the lock. Turning it with hesitation the locked clinked and fell to the ground. Would he unchain her? He hoped not.

It happened too quickly, he could barely scream. She grabbed him by the neck, pushing her claw-like nails under his skin. He could not breathe. The pain started in his abdomen and quickly became him. He tasted metal and he felt himself giving into the red and then the dark. The last thing he ever heard was the crew screaming.
